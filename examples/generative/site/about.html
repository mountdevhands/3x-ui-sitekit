About the collection

Form Sequences

This collection treats a geometric composition as both an image and a set of decisions. Every study includes the actual coordinates, dimensions and colours used to draw it.

Explore, compare, and adapt

The collection contains 3 original parameter-based studies. Each page pairs the finished image with its dimensions, construction data, and source files. Open two studies to compare their proportions or colour choices, then download a starting point for your own variation.

Take a study into your project

Open the SVG in a vector editor to move, resize, or recolour its circles, bars, or frames. The JSON lists each primitive's coordinates and colours, providing a reference when making a related variation.

About the collection

Colour Sets

These studies begin with explicit hue, saturation and lightness steps. The displayed sRGB hex colours, exported CSS variables and measured contrast matrix all come from the same final values.

Explore, compare, and adapt

The collection contains 3 original parameter-based studies. Each page pairs the finished image with its dimensions, construction data, and source files. Open two studies to compare their proportions or colour choices, then download a starting point for your own variation.

Take a study into your project

Copy the supplied --study-color-1 through --study-color-5 CSS variables into a stylesheet and assign them to text, backgrounds, or borders. If you change a hex value, calculate its contrast again before choosing a text and background pair.
